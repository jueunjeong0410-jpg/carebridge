Service 01

Medical coordination

The right doctor, and every step around the visit, arranged.

What this looks like

Before you travel, the practical work begins. We recommend trusted doctors and hospitals for your needs, book your appointments and speak with the hospital on your behalf. On the day, we take care of registration and payment; afterwards, we request your medical documents and help you claim from your insurer. Nothing rushed — simply every detail arranged well in advance.

Included

  • Recommendations of trusted doctors and hospitals for your needs
  • Appointment booking aligned with your travel dates
  • Registration and payment handled on the day of your visit
  • Requests to the hospital for medical records, certificates and other documents
  • Insurance claim support, from paperwork to submission
  • Communication with the hospital before your arrival
  • A written summary of your itinerary and next steps

How it works
