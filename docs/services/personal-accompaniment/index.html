Service 02

Personal accompaniment

An English-speaking companion at the hospital.

What this looks like

Hospitals ask a great deal of attention. A familiar, English-speaking companion stays with you through registration, waiting rooms and consultations, so that the conversation is understood the first time and nothing is left to interpretation afterwards.

Included

  • Accompaniment from arrival through departure each day
  • English interpretation during consultations
  • Support with forms, payments and hospital navigation
  • A short written summary after each visit

How it works

01

We meet before the visit

A short conversation to review the appointment, your questions and anything you hope to raise.

02

We stay with you

Your companion remains present through registration, consultation and any follow-up steps.

03

We close the loop

A brief written summary helps you remember what was discussed and what comes next.

Interpretation and support only. Clinical advice is provided by the treating healthcare professionals.
