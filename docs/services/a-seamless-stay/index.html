Service 03

A seamless stay

Where to stay, how to get there, and what to enjoy in between.

What this looks like

Time in an unfamiliar city should not feel like administration. We shortlist safe accommodation with easy transport and plenty nearby, meet you at the airport, and suggest places to see and restaurants to enjoy before and after your appointments. If you would like a table somewhere, we will book it for you.

Included

  • A shortlist of safe stays with easy transport and plenty to see nearby
  • Airport pick-up on arrival
  • Sightseeing spots to enjoy before and after your appointments
  • Restaurant recommendations around your schedule
  • Restaurant reservations made on your behalf, on request
  • Transfers between your accommodation and the hospital
